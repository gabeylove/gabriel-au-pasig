About Me

My name is Gabriel B. Cabututan, and I live in Pinagbuhatan, Pasig City. I have always been passionate about creativity and technology. Ever since I was young, I have enjoyed playing video games, which introduced me to the world of digital art and storytelling. Over time, I developed a deep interest in creating art, particularly digital illustrations and music covers, allowing me to express myself in unique ways.

Aside from my artistic pursuits, I am also interested in exploring new ideas and learning new skills that help me grow both personally and professionally. I enjoy experimenting with different software tools for digital creation and finding ways to combine my love for art and technology. Whether it's through gaming, music, or design, I am always eager to push my boundaries and refine my craft.
